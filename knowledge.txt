**Shipping Policy:** All orders are processed and shipped within 2 business days after successful order confirmation. Standard shipping usually takes 5 to 7 business days, depending on the customer's delivery location. Express shipping is available for customers who need faster delivery and usually takes 2 business days. Express delivery costs an additional ₹150. Shipping charges and delivery estimates will be displayed during checkout. Once your order has been shipped, you will receive a confirmation message along with tracking details so you can follow your package. Delivery times may vary due to weather conditions, public holidays, courier delays, or other unexpected circumstances.

**Order Tracking:** Once your order has been dispatched, you will receive a tracking number or tracking link through your registered email address or mobile number. You can use the tracking information to check the current status of your shipment. If your tracking details are not updated within 48 hours of receiving the shipping confirmation, please contact our customer support team for assistance.

**Returns Policy:** Customers can request a return within 30 days of receiving their order. To qualify for a return, the product must be unused, unworn, undamaged, and in its original packaging with all tags, labels, accessories, and invoices included. Products showing signs of use, physical damage caused by the customer, or missing original packaging may not qualify for a return. To initiate a return, customers must contact our support team with their order number and reason for requesting the return.

**Refund Policy:** Once we receive your returned product, our team will inspect it to ensure that it meets the return conditions. After successful inspection and approval, the refund will be processed within 7 business days. Refunds will generally be credited to the original payment method used during checkout. Depending on your bank or payment provider, the amount may take additional time to appear in your account. Shipping charges and express delivery fees may be non-refundable unless the return is due to an incorrect, damaged, or defective product.

**Damaged or Incorrect Products:** If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect product, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery. Customers may be required to provide clear photographs or videos showing the issue. After verification, we will arrange a suitable replacement, exchange, or refund depending on product availability and the situation. We aim to resolve such issues as quickly as possible and provide a smooth customer experience.

**Order Cancellation:** Customers can request an order cancellation before the order has been dispatched. Once the product has been shipped, cancellation may not be possible, and customers may need to follow the standard return process after delivery. If a cancellation is approved before shipment, the refund will be processed within 7 business days using the original payment method.

**Warranty Policy:** Selected products may include a manufacturer's warranty depending on the product category. Electronics are covered by a 1-year warranty against manufacturing defects under normal usage conditions. The warranty does not cover accidental damage, water damage, misuse, unauthorized repairs, improper installation, or normal wear and tear. Customers may need to provide proof of purchase to claim warranty services.

**Delivery Issues:** If your order is delayed beyond the estimated delivery date, please contact our customer support team with your order number. If your package is marked as delivered but you have not received it, check with your neighbours, family members, or building reception before contacting us. We will coordinate with our courier partner to investigate the issue.

**Customer Support:** For questions about shipping, returns, refunds, cancellations, or warranty claims, please contact our customer support team through our official email address or contact number listed on our website. Please include your order number and relevant details so that we can assist you efficiently. Our goal is to provide reliable delivery, transparent policies, and a convenient shopping experience for every customer.
